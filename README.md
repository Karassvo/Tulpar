They work on a phone or a computer, with no login or install.

## Open offline

1. Click **Code → Download ZIP** and unzip it.
2. Double-click `index.html` for the e-book or `comic.html` for the comic.

## What's inside

- `index.html`: the e-book. It has 10 chapters, one per course week, with worked examples, Python code, interactive labs and quizzes.
- `comic.html`: the comic *Tulpar-Man: Ten Nights Over Astana*. It has 10 issues, one villain per week, and every syllabus subtopic is drawn in a panel.

AI use: made with help from Claude (Anthropic); the team checked all content. Full declaration inside both files.
